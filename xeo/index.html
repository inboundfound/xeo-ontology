XEO — the ontology for the xEO family

Namespace https://xeoontology.org/xeo# · prefix xeo: · v0.2 · repo · CC BY 4.0

One standards-grounded vocabulary for how engines — search, generative, answer, conversational — see, cite, and serve content. Every term is typed by the kind of claim it makes; the strata run from durable bedrock to interpretive surface:

entityepisodeobservationderivationinterpretationnorm
Modules: xeo-core (substrate) · xeo-visibility (xEO) · xeo-engagement (draft) · xeo-decision (interpretation tier) · xeo-strategy (norms tier) · xeo-align (schema.org / PROV-O / SKOS bridges). Machine-readable Turtle: core, visibility, engagement, decision, strategy, align; JSON-LD context.

Metric Target interpretationxeo-delivery

xeo:MetricTarget

One measurable target of an Objective: metric X should reach targetValue, read as absolute or as a percent lift from a captured baseline. Named a target and never a key result: xeo:Outcome is 'the measured result an experiment produced', an observation, and a goal must not share a name with a reading. Each target is its own node so the experiment loop can hang observations off it; a target dropped on edit is stamped deletedAt, never destroyed, because its id is what measured history is anchored to.

Objective interpretationxeo-delivery

xeo:Objective

What a Campaign intends to move: an operator-written statement in prose (carried verbatim — the recommendation agent reads it as the goal), a resultsWindowDays every target is measured over, and one or more MetricTargets of which exactly one isPrimary. An intent set at creation. A campaign has one reporting horizon, distinct from the Project's work window: a two-month engagement can carry a six-month results window.

Outcome observationxeo-decision

xeo:Outcome

The measured result an experiment produced (lift, delta, significance). The measurement is an observation; any claim that the tactic CAUSED it is an interpretation, carried on the Recommendation, not asserted here.

subclass of
prov:Entity

Playbook entityxeo-strategy

xeo:Playbook

A reusable, discipline-scoped bundle of Diagnostics, Tactics, and Practices — an 'SEO playbook', an 'AEO playbook', a 'local playbook'. Durable and named, like a Diagnostic. Its scope is the DISCIPLINE, not the account: a Playbook is authored once (by the community or an agency) and reused across every client. What varies per client or campaign is which Playbooks a tenancy activates and which Practices it overrides — never the Playbook itself.

Practice normxeo-strategy

xeo:Practice

A standing rule that governs how recommendations are made under stated conditions. Best-practices, SOPs, and lessons-learned are all Practices, distinguished by source, not by class. A Practice does one of three things (practiceKind): veto a candidate (guardrail), reorder it (preference), or attach a caveat (guidance). Revisable and versioned (supersedes); scoped to the concepts it appliesToConcept; audited on the recommendations it fired on (appliedPractice).

Project entityxeo-delivery

xeo:Project

The contractual container for a body of work on one Website. Declares its engagementType (retainer or one-off), an interventionQuota (a scope of work with no ceiling is not a scope), a cadence the quota resets over (retainer) or a durationDays it runs for (one-off), and the Interventions and Tactics inScope under the contract. Never destroyed: its campaigns, objectives and targets are the substrate the end-of-campaign report and the experiment loop read, so it is stamped deletedAt and filtered, not deleted.

hasRole derivationxeo-strategy

xeo:hasRole

The structural role a URL plays on its site, as a slotted-in concept. A recommended starter scheme is coarse and purpose-based: brand-core (home, about), commercial (services, products, sales), content (articles, marketing), infrastructure (pagination, archives, tag and category indexes). Assignment is a DERIVATION from graph and crawl structure — inbound link_position, index-vs-leaf link patterns, template similarity — not URL-string matching; it carries method and confidence, and 'unknown' is a valid, conservative state. Parallels core's inTopic. Norms bind to these roles via appliesToConcept, so 'never redirect an infrastructure page for ranking' is a rule over a role, not a growing list of regexes.
